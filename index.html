Rationale

Why these projects

Collecting information is slow and adds little analytical value. Interpreting it is where the work lies. Can automation shift the balance — without compromising accuracy?

01 Information overload

The supply of information is continuous.

Data, institutional publications and regulatory updates arrive from hundreds of fragmented sources.

02 Verification

Plausible output is not necessarily correct.

A fluent wrong figure gives no cue. Every statistic must be computed and checked, not generated.

03 Interpretation

The value lies in deciding what matters.

Less time reconciling sources means more time on the economic reasoning itself.

04 AI as augmentation

AI enters after validated data, not before.

A constrained language layer only. It is never the source of facts.

Data is retrieved from official sources and validated deterministically. The economic reasoning and the final judgement remain human and traceable to primary sources.
