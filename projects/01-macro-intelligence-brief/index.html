Project 01

Macro Intelligence Brief

Economic data → Structured insight

In Development
Overview

A reproducible weekly reading of the United States macroeconomic picture. The objective is to automate the collection and preparation of the underlying data, compute the conventional economic transformations explicitly, and use a language model only for the final write-up — never for the arithmetic.

Status

In development. The collection, normalisation, calculation and validation stages are implemented and have been run against the live source. The synthesis, brief and review stages do not exist yet, so no brief is produced or distributed, and nothing here runs on a schedule. An earlier prototype produced briefs through a no-code automation platform; that approach has been retired and is preserved only as reference material.

The Problem

Monitoring the macroeconomic picture typically means consulting several sources by hand, retrieving each indicator separately and comparing the latest print with its previous reading. The work is repetitive, time-consuming and adds little analytical value in itself.

It is also easy to do badly. Most published series are levels or indices, not rates, so a naive comparison of two adjacent observations does not produce the statistic an economist would actually quote.

The Experiment

Delegate the collection and computation layers entirely to code, and test whether a language model can turn a set of already-validated statistics into a concise, structured briefing suitable for a first read of the week.

The question is deliberately narrow: not “can a model analyse the economy?”, but “given correct figures, can it describe them accurately and usefully without introducing numbers of its own?”

Intended Method
  1. 01

    Primary Sources

    Official statistical releases and institutional data providers, cited explicitly so every figure can be traced back to its publisher.

  2. 02

    Collection

    Scheduled retrieval of the required series, with the full observation history needed to compute period-over-period and year-over-year transformations.

  3. 03

    Normalisation

    Alignment of frequencies, units, vintages and release dates into one internally consistent dataset.

  4. 04

    Economic Calculations

    Explicit, tested computation of the conventional statistics — inflation rates rather than index levels, annualised growth rather than level differences.

  5. 05

    Validation

    Automated checks on ranges, signs, staleness and internal consistency. A figure that fails validation is not passed downstream.

  6. 06

    AI-Assisted Synthesis

    A language model drafts the narrative from validated figures supplied to it. It does not calculate, infer or retrieve any statistic.

  7. 07

    Structured Brief

    A consistent document structure, with each claim tied to the figure and source underlying it.

  8. 08

    Human Review

    A person reads, corrects and approves the brief before it is considered finished. This step is not optional and is not automated.

Stages 01–05 implemented and run against the live source · stages 06–08 not yet built

Economic Method

A central design rule governs this rebuild: the transformations that turn published series into interpretable statistics are defined, computed and validated in code before any model sees the data.

A consumer price index rising is not the same statement as the inflation rate increasing. A real GDP level differing from the previous quarter is not a conventional growth rate. Each series requires a stated transformation, and the language model is never responsible for producing or inferring one.

Each series now has one stated transformation, implemented as a tested pure function. Changes in rates are reported in percentage points, changes in levels as percentage changes, and the two are never interchanged.

Limitations

This project is an experimental proof of concept. AI-generated text may contain errors and must not replace professional judgement or primary-source verification.

The economic transformations described above are documented as a design rule but are not yet implemented. Until they are, no output from this project should be treated as a reliable reading of any indicator.

Nothing here constitutes investment advice.
